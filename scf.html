Diagnostic Scope

What we look at

Purchasing

Suppliers

Inventory

The Framework

The goal is simple.

Reduce

Rush orders, stockouts, excess inventory, waste, ordering errors, supplier problems, and unnecessary delays.

Save

Purchasing costs, freight costs, emergency-order costs, inventory costs, and wasted employee time.

Improve

Supplier reliability, delivery performance, inventory accuracy, and overall operational visibility.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I get started?

Start with a short conversation. We'll talk through what's happening in your business and confirm the assessment is a good fit before anything else.

What information do I need to provide?

We'll provide a questionnaire and let you know what's relevant. You only need to share information related to the areas being reviewed.

Do I have to work with SCF after the assessment?

No. The assessment stands on its own. If you want help implementing the recommendations, we can discuss implementation options with you.

Do you guarantee savings?

No. We identify potential savings and improvement opportunities based on the information available. Actual results depend on implementation and business conditions.

What types of businesses can use this?

Small businesses that purchase materials, products, inventory, or services from suppliers and deal with purchasing, inventory, supplier, ordering, or delivery challenges.
