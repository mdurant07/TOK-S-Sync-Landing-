FAQ

Common questions

What is S-Sync?

An AI-powered, blockchain-verified platform that brings procurement, logistics, warehouse, transportation, manufacturing, and finance into one verified system of record.

Who is it built for?

Small businesses through enterprise, defense, and higher-education organizations that need reliable visibility into their supply chain and finance operations.

What does "blockchain-verified" mean?

Key records and transactions are logged in a way that's traceable and tamper-evident, so your team can trust the data without manually cross-checking sources.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Most teams start with whichever module solves their most pressing problem, then expand from there.
